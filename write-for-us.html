What We Love

  • ✅ Practical how-tos and tutorials with real examples
  • ✅ Original opinions with a clear, defensible argument
  • ✅ First-hand experience: tools you've actually used
  • ✅ 600–2,000 words — tight enough to finish, deep enough to matter
  • ✅ Clear H2 section structure (easy to scan on mobile)
  • ✅ A strong opening line that earns the scroll
  • ✅ At least one concrete takeaway the reader can act on

What We Won't Publish

  • ❌ Pure promotional pieces or thinly veiled ads
  • ❌ Unedited AI dumps with no human voice or perspective
  • ❌ Generic "top 10 AI tools" lists with no original analysis
  • ❌ Content already published elsewhere (we need first rights)
  • ❌ Misleading claims about AI capabilities or limitations
  • ❌ Articles that exist to build backlinks only
